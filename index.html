✂️ How to make a clip

YouTube can share a 5–60 second Clip of any full game — much better than screen-recording your phone.

Computer:
  1. Open the game video, pause near the moment.
  2. Click Clip (scissors) under the video — may be under “…”.
  3. Drag the handles, name it, Share clip, copy the link.
Phone:
  1. Open in the YouTube app (not the browser).
  2. Tap Clip under the video.
  3. Trim, name, Share clip → Messages / Facebook.

Tip: Veo uploads have chapters — tap “GOAL” under the video to jump right there first.
